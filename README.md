## Professional Summary

In the age of fake news, social media algorithms, and the proliferation of artificial intelligence, it's crucial that people the world over develop their skills in information literacy. As an Information Literacy & Digital Instruction Librarian at a community college in the Chicago metro area, I help a diverse body of students do just that. I am just as passionate an analyst as I am an educator, so I use this space to refine my skills in data gathering, cleansing, analysis, and visualization. My topics of interest include libraries, archives, and the economy.

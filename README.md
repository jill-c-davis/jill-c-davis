## Professional Summary

In the age of fake news, social media algorithms, and AI companions, it's crucial that the public develops skills in information literacy. As an Information Literacy & Digital Instruction Librarian in the Chicago metro area, I help a diverse student body do just that. I am as passionate an analyst as I am an educator, so I use this space to learn about different topics and refine my skills in data gathering, cleansing, analysis, and visualization. My topics of interest include libraries, archives, and the economy. I am also developing a visual novel.
